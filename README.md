## `01` &nbsp; Bosala Technology &nbsp;·&nbsp; [bosala.ps](https://bosala.ps)

> The company itself — a single page, and a particle system doing all the work.

The entire visual core is a hand-written **WebGL2 particle system**, no 3D library involved. The brand mark is drawn to an offscreen canvas, its pixels sampled into tens of thousands of particles, extruded into a three-dimensional tube, and set inside two procedurally generated spiral galaxies. Particles drift on trigonometric turbulence, flee the cursor and spring back, then dissolve and re-form as the page scrolls.

Team portraits run on a second raw-WebGL2 component: a photo plus a grayscale depth map become real-time parallax with depth-multiplied lighting — fake 3D from two flat images.

## `02` &nbsp; C1 &nbsp;·&nbsp; [c1xt.com](https://c1xt.com)

> Most AI logo tools hand you a flat image. **C1 produces real SVG** — vector from the first frame, structured into named parts, editable directly inside the platform. That distinction is the entire product.

AI-powered logo generation and brand identity, with a mascot named **Brushy**.

**Two generation paths.** Quick mode returns a VS pair — two competing directions from a single charge, so the first decision is a choice rather than a re-roll. Visual Identity mode opens a full brief: logo type, design style, mood, audience, palette, slogan.

**Negotiate the brief with AI.** Instead of a blind prompt box, a structured conversation with Claude that proposes *field-level* edits — each one an approve/dismiss card showing exactly what changes and why, with every proposed value re-validated server-side before it reaches you.

**Brand Identity Studio.** The generated SVG is parsed into individual parts you can select, move, rotate, recolor, group and mask on a live canvas — plus a bundled asset library, curated font pairing, and auto-extracted palettes.

**Complete deliverables.** Monochrome, knockout and outline variations, icon-only lockups, favicons measured to a tight square viewport, mockup scenes, and a structured package of SVG, PNG and PDF — zipped in the browser.

**Bilingual throughout.** Full English and Arabic UI with true RTL, down to keyboard shortcuts bound to physical keys so undo still works on an Arabic layout.

## `03` &nbsp; Kitzos &nbsp;·&nbsp; [kitzos.com](https://kitzos.com)

> Keep your data yours — a guarantee the architecture makes, not one the privacy policy makes.

103 free tools across 8 categories — PDF, images, text, dev utilities, calculators, converters, audio. Every one of them runs fully client-side: no uploads, no signup, no backend. Files never leave the device.

A registry-driven design means one entry in a single source of truth generates the page, its SEO metadata, JSON-LD schema, sitemap entry and internal links automatically — adding a tool is adding a record.

## `05` &nbsp; Korvex Tool

> The desktop application that became C1.

A cross-platform desktop app that generates complete brand identity packages for client work. The full pipeline ran locally: a brief wizard, three AI-generated prompt directions reviewed and refined before spending anything, six parallel vector generations, then brand kit assembly and a client-ready ZIP — portfolio PDF, sanitized source SVGs, PNG and JPG exports across light and dark backgrounds, a full favicon set, 18 social variants, palette swatches in ASE, typography pairing, and multi-page brand guidelines. Users could upload their own mockup backgrounds and place the logo with custom anchors, brush and eraser tools. Metadata was stripped from every exported file.

**Why it became C1.** Everything above ran on one machine, for one designer, behind an installer. C1 is that same pipeline rebuilt as a platform: multi-user and account-based, running at the edge instead of on a desktop, with the brand kit turned into a live vector editor rather than a one-shot export — and open to anyone with a browser instead of shipped as a `.exe`.

## `06` &nbsp; Bosala Palestine

> The platform that started it all.

A geo-aware Palestinian business directory built on its own **fully independent map infrastructure** — self-hosted vector tiles, a custom tile-build pipeline from OpenStreetMap and Overture data covering 2.3M buildings, self-hosted routing, and Arabic-first labeling. Built from scratch because no off-the-shelf provider represented Palestinian geography accurately.

It also ran an AI assistant speaking Palestinian Arabic over RAG and hybrid vector search, streaming responses in roughly two seconds, and shipped to Google Play for 19,000+ devices.
